Recommended workflow
1. Draft creation: All operational bank roles can create asset drafts.
2. Draft editing: Authorised users can update asset details and supporting documents.
3. Change approval: Legal / Recovery Officers, Asset Officers, designated Approvers and Bank Administrators can approve or reject changes.
4. Submission: Those same authorised roles can submit approved assets or changes to Surge.
5. Surge review: Surge independently reviews the submission.
One important safeguard
I recommend separating approving a change from approving your own change.

For example:
- An Asset Officer creates an asset and submits it for approval.
- A Legal Officer reviews and approves it.
- Either an authorised Legal Officer or Asset Officer can submit it to Surge.
- If an already-approved asset is modified, its approval status resets and it must go through approval again.
This gives both departments the authority they need while preserving accountability.

One implementation detail: Keep approve_asset_changes and submit_assets_to_surge as separate permissions, even if the same four roles receive both. This gives you flexibility to change institutional policies later without redesigning the permission system.
